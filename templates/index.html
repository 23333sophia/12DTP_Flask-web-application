About BND

, often shortened as BND, is a six-member South Korean boy group formed by KOZ Entertainment, a subsidiary label under HYBE founded by rapper and producer Zico. The group—consisting of members Sungho, Riwoo, Jaehyun, Taesan, Leehan, and Woonhak—debuted on May 30, 2023, with the single album Who!. True to their name, they embrace an approachable, "boy next door" image, creating relatable, easy-listening music about everyday experiences and youth, with several members actively involved in writing and producing their own tracks.

They debuted on May 30, 2023, with their first single album 'WHO!'.
